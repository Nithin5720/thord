Our System

From one piece of
content to a distribution engine.

A six-step system built around strategy, research, production, storytelling, distribution and scaling.

01

Strategy Call

The first step is the strategy call that helps us get to know your business, personality, objectives, audience, and future aspirations.

02

Content & Personality Research

In the second step, we conduct a research on your story, views, lifestyle, and business in order to find out what makes you worth following.

03

Content Production

The third step includes the development and shooting of content specifically tailored to your personality and business.

04

Edit & Storytelling

This is the step when we take your raw videos and edit them into the high quality short-form content through proper editing, hooks, storytelling, and structuring for retention.

05

Multi-Page Distribution

In the fifth step, we distribute your content through multiple pages.

06

Find What Wins & Scale

Finally, we analyze the best performing videos, focus our efforts on them, make more versions, and distribute them.
